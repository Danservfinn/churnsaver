# ChurnSaver Marketing Plan 2026

## Executive Summary

ChurnSaver is an automated payment recovery platform built exclusively for Whop creators. The product transforms failed subscription payments into recovered revenue through intelligent notifications, personalized outreach, and strategic incentives—all on autopilot.

**Current Metrics (from landing page):**
- 97% Recovery Rate
- $2.4M+ Revenue Saved
- 10K+ Recoveries
- 24/7 Automated

## 1. Market Analysis

### Target Audience

**Primary: Whop Creators ($1K-$50K MRR)**

| Segment | Profile | Pain Points | ChurnSaver Value |
|---------|---------|-------------|------------------|
| **Course Creators** | Digital education sellers, $5-15K/mo | 8-12% failed payments, manual follow-up | Automated recovery + templates |
| **Community Owners** | Discord/membership operators | Awkward dunning conversations | Personalized DM outreach |
| **Software Sellers** | SaaS-like products on Whop | No visibility into churn | 365-day analytics |
| **High-Volume Sellers** | $20K+/mo, 500+ subscribers | Recovery at scale is impossible | Unlimited recoveries (Max tier) |

**Secondary: Whop Power Users**
- Creators with multiple products
- Agencies managing Whop storefronts

### Market Size (TAM/SAM/SOM)

```
TAM: ~50,000 active Whop creators
SAM: ~15,000 creators with $1K+ MRR (subscription-based)
SOM: ~1,500 creators (10% penetration in Year 1)

Average churn cost: $500-2,000/mo per creator
ChurnSaver recovery value: $300-1,500/mo saved
```

### Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Target | Price | Whop Integration |
|------------|--------|-------|------------------|
| Churn Buster | General SaaS | $100+/mo | None |
| Gravy | High-volume SaaS | Custom | None |
| Stunning | Stripe users | $50+/mo | None |
| **ChurnSaver** | **Whop creators** | **$0-99/mo** | **Native** |

**Competitive Advantage:** Only payment recovery tool with native Whop integration.
